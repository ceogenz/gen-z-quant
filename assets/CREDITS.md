### Original to this game

"We All Gonna Make It", "Pump & Dump" and "Fifth Ave Glide" play on WAGMI FM (`wagmi-theme.m4a`, `wagmi-pump.m4a`,
`wagmi-glide.m4a`). They were composed as code in `wagmi/radio/build/music/` and are MIT-licensed with the rest of the
game.

## Characters

Quant, Gears, Juno and Fiona (`assets/characters/*.glb`) are original characters made for WAGMI City (invented people)
from [MakeHuman](http://www.makehuman.org)'s base body, clothes and hair (CC0 1.0). Their everyday clips come from the
[CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu) (free for all uses; the data itself may not be
resold; created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217). Quant's freeflow fight moves are Spiderbench's (see below).

## Engine sounds

Quant's car (`assets/sfx/engine/v8-*.m4a`): loops cut at five engine speeds from "Maserati GranTurismo S Exhaust" by
lmartins ([Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maserati_GranTurismo_S_Exhaust.ogg), originally
[Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/lmartins/sounds/465453/)), under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Trimmed, pitch-flattened, looped, levelled and re-encoded.
The gearbox, shifts and pops (`wagmi/audio/supercar.js`) are original.

## The city

The city is built on Slop New York by Mr.doob, under the MIT License (see [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE)). Some city code is
ported from Slop New York's newer commits by Mr.doob, under the same MIT License; each ported part names its commit in a
short header comment.

## Street combat

The street fight's design and code, the Degens' rig and the freeflow clips are ported from **Spiderbench** by Shikhar.
They are used with the author's permission and are **not** covered by the MIT License. `NOTICE` lists exactly which
parts.

## Voices

Quant's, Juno's, Bagsy's, Lev's, Gears', Fiona's, Wick's and Fomo's lines are synthesised with
[Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS) by the Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud (Apache-2.0): each voice was designed
once from a written description and every line cloned from it, run with [mlx-audio](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio)
(MIT). The voices are invented. ZK's lines (and any character's not yet voiced) are placeholders made with the macOS
speech voices; `NOTICE` explains their terms, and `docs/VOICE-LINES.md` explains how to replace them.
